# Adanse Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 17, 2026**

Adanse ("Adanse", "we", "us", or "our") provides a research and thesis
analysis platform that helps students and researchers organize research
projects, analyze datasets, and generate research insights.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it,
how we protect it, and the choices available to you when you use Adanse.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create an Adanse account, we may collect information such as:

-   Email address
-   Name or display name
-   Authentication information
-   Account preferences
-   Information associated with your Adanse account

### Research Projects and Uploaded Data

When you use Adanse, you may provide:

-   Thesis titles and research topics
-   Research objectives and questions
-   Research hypotheses
-   Research datasets
-   Survey or interview data
-   Documents and files
-   Other information you choose to include in a research project

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate rights
and permissions to upload and process this information.

### Usage Information

We may automatically collect information about how you interact with
Adanse, including:

-   Pages and features you use
-   General device and browser information
-   Log information
-   Error and performance information
-   Dates and times of activity

We use this information primarily to operate, secure, maintain, and
improve Adanse.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

-   Provide and operate Adanse
-   Authenticate your account
-   Store and manage your research projects
-   Analyze datasets and research materials at your request
-   Generate reports and research outputs
-   Improve the reliability and performance of the platform
-   Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
-   Communicate with you about your account or the service
-   Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Research Data

Your research data belongs to you.

Adanse processes uploaded datasets and research materials to provide the
features you request.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate rights
and permissions to upload and process information through Adanse.

If your dataset contains personal information about research
participants, you are responsible for obtaining any required consent,
approvals, or other legal authorization before uploading it to Adanse.

Where appropriate, researchers should remove unnecessary personally
identifiable information from datasets before uploading them.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your
information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or
disclosure.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account
credentials and should notify us if you believe your account has been
compromised.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain account information and research materials for as long as
reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account,
comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our
agreements.

You may request deletion of your account and associated information,
subject to legal or operational requirements that may require certain
information to be retained.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Adanse may rely on third-party providers for services such as:

-   Authentication
-   Cloud infrastructure
-   Database and storage services
-   Analytics
-   Payment processing

These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected
to handle information according to applicable contractual and legal
requirements.

## 7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Adanse may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to:

-   Keep you signed in
-   Maintain sessions
-   Remember preferences
-   Understand how the service is used
-   Improve security and performance

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings.
Disabling certain technologies may affect the functionality of Adanse.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights
relating to your personal information, including the right to:

-   Request access to your information
-   Request correction of inaccurate information
-   Request deletion of certain information
-   Request restriction of certain processing
-   Object to certain processing
-   Request a copy of certain information

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the contact
information provided on the Adanse website.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Adanse is intended for university students, researchers, academics, and
other users who are legally permitted to use the service.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in
violation of applicable law.

## 10. International Data Processing

Adanse and its service providers may process or store information in
countries other than the country in which you live.

Where required, we will take appropriate measures for such transfers in
accordance with applicable law.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make material changes, we may notify users through the service
or other appropriate means.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy indicates when the
policy was most recently revised.

## 12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information,
please contact Adanse through the contact information provided on
**adanse.app**.

### Important Notice

Adanse is a research assistance and analysis platform. It does not
replace qualified academic supervisors, statisticians, research ethics
committees, or other professionals.

You are responsible for reviewing and verifying outputs generated by
Adanse before relying on them.
